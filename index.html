About our upload tool

Our videos are produced with an internal tool that writes scripts, adds narration and licensed stock footage, and uploads the finished videos to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. The tool is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public. It does not access any other user's data.
