Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wonderloop upload tool ("the App") handles data. The App is an internal tool operated by the owner of the Wonderloop YouTube channel. It is used solely to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and is not available to other users.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's collection and use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

Through Google OAuth 2.0, the App requests permission to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the authorized YouTube channel (youtube.upload);
  • read basic channel information, such as the channel title, to confirm which channel is connected (youtube.readonly).

The App does not access, collect or analyze data about YouTube viewers, subscribers, comments or any other users.

How information is used

Authorization is used only to publish videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails created for the Wonderloop channel, and to confirm the connected channel name.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer or private server. It is not stored on any public server and is never shared, sold or disclosed to third parties. No cookies or tracking technologies are used by the App. This website does not collect personal information.

Data retention and deletion

The App stores only the OAuth token and local records of videos it has uploaded (video ID, title and upload time). The token is deleted when access is revoked or the App is uninstalled. Local upload records can be deleted at any time, and will be deleted within 7 days of a request.

Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the App can no longer access the YouTube account.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the channel owner through the email listed on the Wonderloop YouTube channel's About page.
